Our Story

From running the books to running transformation

Sterling grew out of a simple track record: years spent managing the financial operations behind our clients' businesses — from finance and accounting to real-asset and property portfolios — so they could focus on driving commercial growth.

That operator's DNA still defines us. But the demands on financial services have moved on: tighter margins, higher expectations, and the arrival of AI as a genuine operating capability. So we've broadened — from back-office finance into strategy, operational transformation, leadership advisory and AI enablement, across Europe, Asia, the Middle East and Africa.

The through-line is unchanged. We bring insight, expertise and impact — and we stay for delivery.
